Chicken Biryani

A delicious and flavorful Indian-style chicken biryani made with aromatic spices and basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, salt and biryani masala.
  3. Heat oil in a large pan and fry the onions until golden brown.
  4. Add tomatoes and marinated chicken. Cook until the chicken is almost completely cooked.
  5. Add the soaked rice and enough water. Mix gently.
  6. Cover the pan and cook on low heat until the rice is completely cooked.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander and mint leaves.
  8. Serve hot with raita or your favorite side dish.

🍽️ Enjoy your delicious Chicken Biryani!
